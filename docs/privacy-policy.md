cookandpin is an offline recipe app. It includes its recipes and images in the download. You can browse without creating an account.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves your pinned recipes, selected shopping recipes, ingredient checkmarks, manually added shopping item text and checkmarks, and chosen recipe portions in its private app storage. This lets your choices remain after you close the app. The current app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, camera, or payment details. It does not include advertising or an analytics service. It does not upload your recipe choices to the developer's server.

If device backup is enabled, Android may back up those saved choices to your Google account and restore them when you reinstall or move devices. Android may also transfer them directly between devices. Google's backup service handles that data under Google's terms and your device settings; the developer does not receive access to it through the app. On some Android versions, the backup protection depends on your device security settings.

## Sharing and retention

The app does not share your choices with the developer or advertisers. If you choose to open a recipe source link, your browser opens that external website; it may receive usual browser request information under its own privacy policy. The app does not send your shopping list or pins with the link. The Android backup and device transfer described above are controlled by your device settings. Saved choices remain in the app until you change or remove them or clear the app's storage. Uninstalling or clearing storage deletes the local copy; a backup copy may remain in your Google account until it is replaced or removed according to your device's backup settings. cookandpin has no user accounts to delete.

## Your choices

You can unpin recipes, remove recipes and manual items from the shopping list, and change portions in the app. To erase all local saved choices, clear cookandpin's app storage in Android Settings. You can turn Android backup on or off in your device settings. For questions about this policy, use the privacy contact above.

## Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form will be updated before the changed version is released.
